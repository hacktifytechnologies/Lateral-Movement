## MCQ 1
T1534 Internal Spearphishing differs from T1566 External Spearphishing because:
A) Internal phishing uses email only  B) It originates from a compromised internal account, making it more trusted and likely to bypass email security controls  ✅

## MCQ 2

A) Guaranteed success  B) Human execution of the payload on their behalf — bypassing technical controls and leaving the victim's account in process trees  ✅
C) No network traffic  D) Bypassing all authentication

## MCQ 3
Defending against T1534 when a trusted internal account is already compromised requires:
A) Blocking all internal email  B) User security awareness training + anomaly detection on internal email patterns + mail gateway scanning  ✅
C) Disabling email entirely  D) Requiring 2FA only for external emails

## Fill 1
The primary reason internal phishing is more convincing than external phishing:
**Answer:** The email comes from a trusted internal domain/account that employees recognize

## Fill 2
MITRE technique for the initial compromise of the internal account used for T1534:
**Answer:** Any initial access technique (e.g., T1566 External Phishing, T1078 Valid Accounts)
