## Fill 1
Registry value that enables/disables RDP on Windows (0=enabled, 1=disabled):
**Answer:** `HKLM\System\CurrentControlSet\Control\Terminal Server\fDenyTSConnections`

## Fill 2
`xfreerdp` flag to ignore TLS certificate warnings during red team ops:
**Answer:** `/cert:ignore` (or `/cert-ignore`)
